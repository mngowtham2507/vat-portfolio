09 / Questions

Asked often.

What does VAT Studio do?

It is the creative and digital studio of Vyara Amoghya Technologies in Bengaluru. It covers brand design, websites, apps, custom software, AI chatbots and automation, marketing, UI/UX, and photo and video production, all handled by one team.

Where are you based, and who do you work with?

We are based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, and work with businesses across India. Clients are mainly startups, small and mid-sized businesses and growing enterprises in real estate, hospitality, healthcare, retail, manufacturing and professional services.

How are packages structured?

Each service has three fixed tiers. Starter suits small businesses and early-stage startups. Growth adds customisation, integrations and brand-specific design. Enterprise covers multi-location or complex needs. Scope and timeline are shown before you sign, and quotes are available on request.

Can you build an AI chatbot for a website or WhatsApp?

Yes. We start with a session to find where an assistant would save the most time, then set it up and connect it to your website or WhatsApp. One round of flow revisions and a basic analytics dashboard are included. Larger plans add channels and training on your own data.

How do I start a project?

Send a brief through the form below, by email, or by phone or WhatsApp. We first learn your goals, audience and existing systems, then agree scope, priorities and a delivery plan. You speak with real people.
